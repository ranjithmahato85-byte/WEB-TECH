Key System Features

Built to uphold democratic integrity, voter privacy, and administrative transparency

Ballot Secrecy Protocol

Individual selections are decoupled from voter identity in the database. Even system administrators cannot trace which candidate a voter chose.

✓

Voter ID & Ward Verification

Eligibility is verified against Nepal's 7 provinces, 77 districts, and municipal ward allocations before a ballot can be cast.

◉

Real-time Transparent Tally

Automated tabulation removes human counting errors, with clear turnout metrics and candidate percentage distributions.

How The System Works

A four-stage process designed for every citizen

1

Register

Sign up with your citizenship or voter ID, provincial address, and local ward.

2

Browse

Review active elections, candidate biographies, and certified party symbols.

3

Vote

Select a candidate, confirm your choice on screen, and cast a secret ballot.

4

Verify

Receive a tamper-evident confirmation number while your choice stays secret.
